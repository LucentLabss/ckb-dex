# DEx — Weekly Development Update

## Overview

Over the past week, we consolidated our development efforts around the DEx.

The DEx development has been split into four major components, listed below in order of priority:

1. **Onchain Scripts**
2. **Offchain**
3. **Backend Matching Engine**
4. **Frontend**

> **Note:** Items 1, 2, and 3 are being developed asynchronously.

---

## Current PoC

The current iteration is a **Proof of Concept (PoC) orderbook DEx**.

The PoC allows users to:

- Place **bids** (buy orders)
- Place **asks** (sell orders)
- Have trades matched automatically by a **real-time matching engine**

The objective at this stage is to validate the core orderbook architecture and its interaction with the CKB onchain infrastructure before expanding into the final product.

---

## Development Priorities

### 1. Onchain Scripts

The highest-priority component is the development of the onchain scripts.

Current focus:

- Buyer/bidding scripts
- Order execution logic
- Interaction between onchain scripts and the backend
- Documentation of the PoC's onchain architecture

### 2. Offchain

The offchain layer is being developed alongside the onchain scripts.

The focus is on establishing the transaction-building and offchain logic required to interact with the onchain contracts.

### 3. Backend Matching Engine

The backend matching engine will provide automated real-time matching between bids and asks.

During the past week, the backend architecture was formalized, including:

- Database models
- Database schemas
- Core backend interactions
- Interaction between the backend and onchain scripts

The architecture diagram is available here:

[Excalidraw Architecture Diagram](https://excalidraw.com/#json=rJb7XT9jz1HinvW5YGMdh,DLU2KuioLEAskP3ensB5VA)

### 4. Frontend

Frontend development is currently the lowest priority while the core onchain, offchain, and backend components are being implemented.

## Future Product Direction

> **Note:** The following is outside the scope of the current PoC.

While implementing the PoC, we are also researching the architecture of the eventual finished DEx.

One of the main decisions under consideration is whether the final product should be:

1. A fully **orderbook-based DEx**, or
2. A **hybrid DEx** combining an AMM with an orderbook.

This is currently in the research phase.

The research is being guided by the key points and guardrails previously shared with the team, particularly around what the wider team and community are most likely to welcome and appreciate.

## Summary

The immediate priority remains validating the core orderbook DEx through the PoC.

The development sequence is:

**Onchain Scripts → Offchain → Matching Engine → Frontend**

The onchain, offchain, and backend components are being developed asynchronously, with this week's primary focus on implementing the backend architecture, matching engine, buyer-side bidding scripts, and documenting the onchain architecture.
